In 2008, a significant cyber-attack occurred on Wells Fargo. A third-party vendor credential leak allowed unauthorized access to customer records held by an external partner. A third party data share leaked customer information. An unrotated vendor key remained valid for too long. A vendor credential leak enabled unauthorized access. The breach exposed approximately 200,000 records. Wells Fargo disclosed the incident after an internal investigation of several months.
