In 2018, a significant cyber-attack occurred on Marriott. Unauthorized access persisted for years in an acquired reservation database, exposing guest records including passport numbers. Unauthorized access persisted undetected for a long period. The intruders accessed a guest reservation database. The breach exposed passport numbers of guests. A prolonged dwell time allowed extensive data theft. The breach exposed approximately 383,000,000 records. Marriott disclosed the incident only after a prolonged delay, drawing regulatory criticism. The incident response team investigated the intrusion and mitigated the threat. Marriott later strengthened its access control controls.
